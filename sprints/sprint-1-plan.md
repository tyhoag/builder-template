# Sprint 1 Plan

**Goal:** Get early signal from interviews with mentors in Colombia, plus a working WhatsApp prototype for them to react to, so we can judge whether Mentors International's entrepreneurs would use a WhatsApp teaching curriculum.

**Why this:** The team already has a PRD and several Claude artifact mock-ups. The next step is something actionable for the Mentors International executive team ahead of a pilot in Colombia or Mexico: a prototype that can actually be used in WhatsApp, and early signal from the people it is for.

**Done looks like:** A test WhatsApp number (built on Meta's API) that anyone can message, in text or by voice memo, to get lesson 1, and that replies in text. Lesson 1 comes from content the team already wrote from Mentors International's documents (under NDA, so not in this repo). I also join some of the mentor interviews in Colombia, which the product managers and the international relationship lead run.

**Predicted difficulty:** 4
